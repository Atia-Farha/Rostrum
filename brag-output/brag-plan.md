# Brag Plan: Rostrum

## What is this app?
Rostrum is an AI debate training platform that acts as an on-demand sparring partner, live argument coach, and formal adjudicator — letting debaters speak aloud through structured rounds and receive 100-point rubric reports in both English and Bangla.

## The angle
This isn't a generic chatbot that answers debate questions. Rostrum is a full competitive debate arena in your browser: you **speak aloud**, an AI opponent with a distinct persona and voice **argues back in character** using phase- and slot-aware stage briefs, and a formal judge **scores you on the exact Matter, Manner, and Method rubric** used in university championships — while transparently noting where the platform still has rough edges.

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
**"Competitive debate requires three roles: a sparring partner, a coach, and a judge. Between club sessions, debaters have none of them."**

## Intended Users
- **University & School Debaters** preparing solo for Asian Parliamentary (AP) and World Schools (WSDC) tournaments
- **Bangla Circuit Participants** needing bilingual motions, native-script debate turns, and Bangla adjudication
- **Public Speaking & Logic Learners** practicing structured reasoning under timed pressure

## Key Moments & Features to Demonstrate
1. **6-Step Debate Setup (`/dashboard/setup`)** — Language (English / বাংলা), Motion (manual or Gemini-generated by category), Side (Government / Opposition / Random), 3 AI Personas (Calm Logician, Aggressive Cross-Examiner, Devil's Advocate), 4 Difficulty tiers (Beginner to World Champion), and Tournament vs Sparring format.
2. **Live Debate Arena (`/debates/{id}`)** — Real split-pane workspace: transcript feed on the left with phase dividers and speech bubbles; podium on the right with mic orb, phase clock, AI Stack attribution, and phase stepper.
3. **Mid-Debate Argument Rewriting** — Tapping "Improve argument" on any user turn generates a cached, slot-aware "Enhanced Version" with "What changed" coaching bullets without altering the scored transcript.
4. **Formal Adjudication Report (`/debates/{id}/feedback`)** — "77 out of 100 · Strong" cover verdict, "The short version" brief, Matter (/40), Manner (/30), Method (/30) scorecard with per-criterion ladders, grouped findings with verified transcript quotes, logical fallacy detection, delivery time analysis, and PDF export.
5. **Overview Dashboard & History (`/dashboard/overview`, `/dashboard/history`)** — Account-scoped metric strip, Score Trend SVG chart, Rubric Averages, Reports by Difficulty, and searchable/filterable session history.
6. **Bilingual Parity & Fallback Chain** — End-to-end English & Bangla debates backed by ElevenLabs Scribe STT → Gemini multimodal fallback, Gemini 3.6 Flash, and ElevenLabs TTS → Google Translate TTS → text-only fallback.
7. **Incomplete Features & Known Limitations** — Honestly identified from the codebase:
   - **Bangla TTS Phonetics**: ElevenLabs `eleven_multilingual_v2` can sound unnaturally accented in Bengali (text transcripts remain clear and accurate).
   - **Report Scaffolding Localization**: In Bangla debates, the judge's findings, verdict, fallacies, and transcript are Bangla, while PHP-derived scaffolding (the 4-line brief labels, grade ladder summaries, phase notes, and delivery commentary) remains English-only.
   - **Browser Capture Constraint**: Voice recording uses HTML5 `MediaRecorder` (`audio/webm`), targeting Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave); Safari and Firefox fall back with a browser notice.
   - **Free-Tier Hosting & API Dependencies**: Render free-tier cold starts and external Gemini/ElevenLabs credit limits (mitigated by fallback chains, not full offline AI).
